What this site does

To make our directory more useful than a raw list, we do the following:

  • Cross-reference Medicare Care Compare for Medicare-certified home health agencies. Federally-published quality ratings, patient survey results, and complaint history are linked from agency detail pages where applicable.
  • Capture state licensing numbers from upstream data feeds and our internal records when available, and surface them in the agency detail page so families can verify the license against the state regulator's lookup.
  • Link the state licensing authority for every state and DC, on every agency detail page, so families can perform the lookup with a single click. The full state-by-state directory of regulators lives in our verification guide.
  • Retire listings flagged for inaccuracy by users via our contact form.
  • Re-validate the catalog against an internal categorical-relevance script when source data refreshes — listings that match institutional or residential venue patterns (assisted living, nursing home, retirement community, treatment center, retail) are noindexed rather than presented as in-home care providers.
  • Disclose AI-assisted content where applicable. Some agency descriptions are restored from upstream marketing data; others are generated from structured agency data using an AI model with explicit anti-fabrication and anti-rating-claim constraints. See our editorial standards for the full disclosure.

What this site does not do

We do not verify agencies on an ongoing basis. State regulators do that — that's their job, and they're better at it. We do not endorse specific agencies; inclusion in the directory is not a recommendation. We do not collect or pass leads to agencies. We are not paid by listed agencies for placement, ranking, or featured status.

The verification work — license lookup, reference calls, caregiver-vetting questions, contract review — is the family's responsibility before engaging any agency. Our how to verify a home care agency guide is the framework we'd recommend using.
